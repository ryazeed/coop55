Privacy notice

E-COOP stores the personal data needed to run co-op training (name, contact details, student ID, major, GPA, CV, attendance, reports and evaluations) in line with the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law. Only people involved in your placement can see your data. Files are stored outside the public website and every sensitive action is logged.

What we collect

Name, email, phone, student ID, major, GPA, CV, attendance check-ins, final reports, evaluations, and messages you send in E-COOP.

How we use it

Only to run co-op training: matching students with companies, recording attendance, reviewing reports and recording evaluations.

Who can see it

The company you applied to, your academic supervisor and the co-op office. Files are stored outside the public website and every access to sensitive actions is logged.

How long we keep it

For the duration of your studies and as required by college regulations. Daily backups are kept for 30 days.

Your rights

You can see and correct your data in your profile and settings, and ask the co-op office for a copy or for deletion where regulations allow.
